H6 (Revenue per market_year using join)
SELECT mdi.market_year,
       ROUND(SUM(cp.quantity * cp.cost_to_customer_per_qty), 2) AS total_revenue
FROM market_date_info mdi
JOIN customer_purchases cp
ON mdi.market_date = cp.market_date
GROUP BY mdi.market_year
ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;

13. Filtered Groups: Calculate the average product price per category, but ONLY include products that have a non-null size and a price greater than 0.
-> select pc.product_category_id,pc.product_category_name,avg(vi.original_price) as "Average_price"
     from vendor_inventory vi 
join product p 
on p.product_id = vi.product_id
join product_category pc
on pc.product_category_id=p.product_category_id

where p.product_size is not null and vi.original_price > 0
group by pc.product_category_id,pc.product_category_name;
