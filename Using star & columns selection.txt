Question 2: Vendor Contact List
We need to generate a simple calling list. Retrieve only the vendor_name, vendor_owner_first_name, and vendor_owner_last_name from the vendor table.

Query:
select vendor_name, vendor_owner_first_name, vendor_owner_last_name
from vendor;

Question 3: Customer Zip Codes
To map our demographic reach, select just the customer_zip column from the customer table.

Query:
select customer_zip
from customer;

Question 4: Product Sizes
Select the product_name and its corresponding product_size from the product table.

Query:
select product_name, product_size
from product;
