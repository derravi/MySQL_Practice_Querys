(1) Get the price of the most expensive item per vendor?
-> select vendor_id,max(original_price) as "Expensive_price" from vendor_inventory
group by vendor_id;

(2) Rank the products in each vendor’s inventory. Expensive products get a lower rank.
-> select product_id,vendor_id,original_price ,
rank() over (partition by vendor_id order by original_price desc) as "Price_rank"
from vendor_inventory;

(3) As a farmer, you want to figure out which of your products were above the average price per product on each market date?
-> select * from (select vendor_id,market_date,product_id,original_price,
avg(original_price) over(partition by market_date) as "Average_price"
from vendor_inventory) as a
where original_price>Average_price
