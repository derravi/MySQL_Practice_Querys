 MATH FUNCTIONS (ROUND / MODULO / CALCULATIONS)
============================================================

Q4. Total spend = quantity * cost_to_customer_per_qty (rounded 2 decimals).
Your Answer:
select *,round((quantity*cost_to_customer_per_qty),2) as "Total Cost" from customer_purchases;

Q10. Original price rounded to 0 decimals.
Your Answer:
select round(original_price,0) from vendor_inventory;

Q12. Total cost for Vendor ID 7 purchases.
Your Answer:
select *,round((quantity*cost_to_customer_per_qty),2) as "total_cost" from customer_purchases;

Corrected (total for vendor 7):
select round(sum(quantity*cost_to_customer_per_qty),2) as total_cost
from customer_purchases
where vendor_id = 7;

Q16. Even/odd using modulo 2.
Your Answer:
select customer_id,case when customer_id%2 then "Even" else "Odd" end as "Reminder" from customer;

Corrected:
select customer_id,
case when customer_id % 2 = 0 then "Even" else "Odd" end as "Result"
from customer;

Q22. Square of quantity.
Your Answer:
select *,(quantity*quantity) as "Power" from customer_purchases;

Q28. Sale Price = original_price / 2.
Your Answer:
select (original_price/2) as "Sale_Price" from vendor_inventory;

Q30. Items where quantity*original_price > 50.
Your Answer:
select *, (quantity*original_price) as "Value" from vendor_inventory
where (quantity*original_price) > 50;

Q34. Remainder when product_id divided by 5.
Your Answer:
select *,(product_id%5) as "Reminder" from product;

Q40. Revenue = quantity*original_price rounded to 1 decimal.
Your Answer:
select round((quantity*original_price),1) as "Revenue" from vendor_inventory;

Q46. Cube of quantity.
Your Answer:
select *,round((quantity*quantity*quantity),2) as "Cube" from customer_purchases;

Q52. New product_id = product_id + 10.
Your Answer:
select *,(product_id+10) as "New_Product_id" from product;

Q58. Difference between cost_to_customer_per_qty and 5.
Your Answer:
select *, round((cost_to_customer_per_qty-5),2) as "New_ctcpq" from customer_purchases;
